What not to force

DateTime format-preset getters are constant aliases. Calling every getter raises a percentage without protecting behavior.

Debug-only util.inspect.custom hooks remain untested, following the suite's established prototype-access pattern without introducing Node-specific inspect calls.

Unreachable branches include the non-finite diff day delta for valid DateTimes and the Duration#as("__proto__") compatibility fallback. Document them rather than fabricate internal objects or lock a known prototype bug into Jest.

Legacy formatToParts shims, old Intl.Locale.weekInfo variants, source-map artifacts, and the unreachable Interval#splitAt clamp belong in a compatibility matrix or narrow exclusion.
